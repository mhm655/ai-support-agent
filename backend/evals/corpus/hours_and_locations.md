# Brightleaf Family Dental - Hours, Locations and Getting Here

Brightleaf Family Dental operates two offices in the metro area. The two
locations keep different schedules, so please check the one you are booked
at rather than assuming they match.

## Riverside office

The Riverside office is our main location and handles the majority of
general dentistry appointments.

Address: 1420 Riverside Parkway, Suite 210, Brightleaf, NC 27701.

Riverside office hours:

- Monday: 8:00am to 5:00pm
- Tuesday: 8:00am to 5:00pm
- Wednesday: 8:00am to 7:00pm
- Thursday: 8:00am to 5:00pm
- Friday: 8:00am to 3:00pm
- Saturday: 9:00am to 2:00pm, first and third Saturday of each month only
- Sunday: closed

The Wednesday evening block until 7:00pm was added specifically for patients
who cannot take time off during the working day. Those slots fill roughly
three weeks in advance, so book early if you need one.

Riverside Saturday hours run only on the first and third Saturday of the
month. On those Saturdays we staff two hygienists and one dentist, which
means we can see cleanings and simple fillings but not crowns, implants or
oral surgery. If a Saturday falls on a holiday weekend the office is closed
and the Saturday rotation shifts to the following week.

## Oak Park office

The Oak Park office is smaller and is where our pediatric and orthodontic
appointments are concentrated.

Address: 88 Oak Park Avenue, Brightleaf, NC 27705.

Oak Park office hours:

- Monday: closed
- Tuesday: 9:00am to 6:00pm
- Wednesday: 9:00am to 6:00pm
- Thursday: 9:00am to 6:00pm
- Friday: 9:00am to 4:00pm
- Saturday: closed
- Sunday: closed

Oak Park is closed every Monday. This is the single most common scheduling
mistake patients make, because the Riverside office is open on Mondays.
If you booked online and are unsure which office your appointment is at,
the confirmation email names the location in the subject line.

## Phones and messages

The front desk phone line, (919) 555-0142, is answered during the hours of
the Riverside office, including on the Saturdays when Riverside is open.
Voicemail left outside those hours is returned on the next business day,
usually before noon.

Requests sent through the contact form on our website are answered within
one business day. The contact form is not monitored overnight and should
never be used for anything urgent. For urgent problems outside office hours,
see the emergency care document.

## Holiday closures

Both offices are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day,
Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after Thanksgiving, and from
December 24 through January 1 inclusive for the winter break.

Both offices close at 1:00pm on Christmas Eve if it falls on a weekday, and
on New Year's Eve. The winter break closure is the longest of the year, and
we recommend refilling any prescriptions and scheduling any needed follow-up
before December 20.

We do not close for federal holidays that are not listed above. Columbus Day,
Veterans Day and Presidents Day are all normal working days at both offices.

## Parking and access

The Riverside office has free patient parking in the attached deck on levels
two and three. Bring your ticket to the front desk for validation; without
validation the deck charges four dollars per hour. Street parking on
Riverside Parkway is metered and enforced until 6:00pm on weekdays.

Oak Park has a small surface lot with fourteen spaces directly in front of
the building, and overflow parking is permitted in the shared lot behind the
pharmacy next door after 10:00am.

Both offices are on the ground floor or served by elevator and are fully
wheelchair accessible. The Riverside suite is on the second floor and the
elevator is immediately to the left of the main lobby entrance. Accessible
parking spaces are on level two of the Riverside deck, nearest the elevator
lobby, and in the two marked spaces closest to the door at Oak Park.

## Public transit

The number 6 and number 11 bus routes both stop within one block of the
Riverside office, at the Riverside Parkway and Fourth Street stop. The Oak
Park office is a twelve minute walk from the Brightleaf commuter rail
station, or a four minute ride on the number 3 bus.

## Arriving for your appointment

We ask patients to arrive ten minutes before the scheduled appointment time,
and fifteen minutes before if it is a first visit and forms have not been
completed in advance. Arriving more than ten minutes late may mean the
appointment has to be shortened or rescheduled, depending on what is booked
after you. See the policies document for how late arrivals and no-shows are
handled.
