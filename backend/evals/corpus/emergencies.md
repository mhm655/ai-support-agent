# Brightleaf Family Dental - Emergency and After-Hours Care

## What counts as a dental emergency

Treat the following as urgent and call us the same day:

- Severe or throbbing toothache that is not controlled by over the counter
  painkillers
- Facial swelling, especially swelling around the eye or under the jaw
- A tooth that has been knocked out completely
- A tooth that has been pushed out of position or is loose after an injury
- Uncontrolled bleeding from the mouth that does not stop after twenty
  minutes of firm pressure
- A broken tooth with sharp edges cutting the tongue or cheek, or with the
  nerve exposed

The following are not emergencies and can wait for a normal appointment: a
lost filling with no pain, a temporary crown that has come off, a chipped
tooth that is not sharp and does not hurt, food caught between teeth, and
mild sensitivity to cold.

## Swelling and difficulty breathing

Facial swelling that is spreading, swelling that closes the eye, difficulty
swallowing or difficulty breathing is a medical emergency, not a dental one.
Go to the nearest emergency department or call 911. Do not wait for a dental
appointment. Dental infections in the floor of the mouth can progress
quickly, and this is the one situation where we would rather you skipped
calling us entirely and went straight to hospital.

## A knocked-out tooth

A permanent tooth that has been knocked out can often be saved if it is
replanted within an hour, so this is genuinely time critical.

Pick the tooth up by the crown, the chewing surface, and never by the root.
If it is dirty, rinse it briefly in milk or saline, or in the saliva of the
patient. Do not scrub it, do not use soap, and do not let it dry out.

If you can, push the tooth gently back into the socket and hold it there by
biting on a clean cloth. If you cannot, put the tooth in a cup of cold milk,
or in saline. Water is a poor choice and damages the root surface cells, so
use it only if there is nothing else.

Then call us immediately. Baby teeth are not replanted, so if it is a first
tooth of a child do not attempt to reinsert it, but still call us.

## During office hours

Call (919) 555-0142 and say clearly that it is an emergency. We hold two
urgent slots each weekday morning at the Riverside office and we will fit
you in the same day wherever we possibly can. An emergency exam is one
hundred and forty five dollars and covers diagnosis, an x-ray of the tooth
and immediate relief of pain; any definitive treatment is quoted separately.

## Outside office hours

Call the main number, (919) 555-0142, and follow the prompt for the on-call
dentist. The on-call service pages a dentist, who returns the call, usually
within thirty minutes. This line is staffed overnight, at weekends and on
public holidays including the winter break closure.

The on-call dentist will decide by phone whether you need to be seen that
night or whether it can wait until morning. Most calls are resolved with
advice. If you are asked to come in after hours, an after-hours surcharge of
two hundred dollars applies on top of the treatment fee.

Do not use the website contact form or email for an emergency. Neither is
monitored outside office hours.

## Managing pain until you are seen

Ibuprofen is more effective than paracetamol for dental pain because most
dental pain is inflammatory. Taking both together, at their normal doses and
spaced as directed on the packet, works better than either alone. Follow the
dosing on the packaging and do not exceed it.

Do not place aspirin directly against the gum or the tooth. It does not help
and it causes a chemical burn of the soft tissue.

A cold compress on the outside of the cheek, twenty minutes on and twenty
minutes off, helps with swelling after an injury. Heat should not be used on
a swelling that might be infected.

Clove oil applied to a small piece of cotton wool can dull the pain of an
exposed nerve temporarily. It is a stopgap and not a treatment.

We do see emergency patients who are not registered with the practice,
subject to availability, at the same emergency exam fee. You will be asked
to complete new patient forms at the visit and payment is due on the day,
