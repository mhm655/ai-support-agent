# Brightleaf Family Dental - New Patient Information

## Booking your first visit

New patients can book by phone at (919) 555-0142, through the request form on
our website, or by walking into either office during opening hours. Online
requests are confirmed by a phone call, because the first visit needs a
longer slot than the online system can reserve on its own.

The wait for a new patient appointment is typically two to three weeks at
Riverside and one to two weeks at Oak Park. If you have pain, say so when
you call. We hold two urgent slots each weekday morning at Riverside for
patients in pain, and those are not visible in the online booking system.

We are currently accepting new patients at both offices, including children.

## What the first visit involves

Plan for about ninety minutes. The first visit includes a full set of x-rays,
a comprehensive exam by the dentist, a gum assessment with pocket depth
measurements charted for every tooth, an oral cancer screening, and a
discussion of anything you want to raise.

A cleaning is not automatically part of the first visit. If your gums are
healthy we will usually do the cleaning on the same day when time allows,
but if the gum assessment shows periodontal disease, the right treatment is
scaling and root planing rather than a routine cleaning, and that is booked
separately. We would rather tell you this in advance than surprise you in
the chair.

## What to bring

Bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card, and a list of any medications
you take including the doses. If you have had x-rays taken elsewhere in the
last two years, ask that office to send them to us; that often saves you
taking a new set. If you take a blood thinner or a bisphosphonate, or you
have had a joint replacement or heart valve surgery, tell us before the
appointment rather than on the day, because those can change how we plan
treatment.

Bring a parent or legal guardian if the patient is under eighteen and it is
a first visit.

## Paperwork

New patient forms can be completed online through the link in your
confirmation email, and doing so in advance saves about fifteen minutes at
the office. The forms cover your medical history, your dental history, the
privacy acknowledgement and the financial policy.

If you prefer paper, arrive twenty minutes early and the front desk will
give you a clipboard. The forms are also available as a PDF on the website
if you would rather print and complete them at home.

## Transferring from another dentist

Ask your previous office to send records and x-rays directly to us, or sign
the release form we provide and we will request them on your behalf. Most
offices send records within one to two weeks. You do not need to wait for
records to arrive before your first appointment; we will take our own x-rays
if yours have not arrived.

There is no fee to transfer in, and no obligation to continue treatment
planned by a previous dentist. It is entirely reasonable to want a second
opinion on a treatment plan, and we will give one without pressure to switch.

## Second opinions

We offer second opinion consultations for one hundred and ten dollars, which
includes reviewing your existing records and x-rays and a twenty minute
discussion with a dentist. If you decide to have the treatment done here,
the consultation fee is credited against the cost of that treatment.

## Recall and reminders

After the first visit we will put you on a recall schedule, most often six
months, and you will be offered the chance to book that appointment before
you leave. Patients who book at checkout are far more likely to actually be
seen on time, so we always ask.

## Language and accessibility

Two of our front desk staff speak Spanish, and both offices can arrange a
telephone interpreter for other languages with two business days notice at
no cost to you.

If you need an ASL interpreter, tell us when booking and we will arrange one;
this needs at least five business days notice. Written materials can be
provided in large print on request.
