# Brightleaf Family Dental - About the Practice and the Team

## The practice

Brightleaf Family Dental opened in 2009 as a single operatory practice on
Riverside Parkway and now runs eleven chairs across two offices. We are an
independent practice, owned by the dentists who work here, and are not part
of a corporate dental chain. Decisions about treatment are made by the
dentist and the patient, and there are no production quotas or bonus targets
tied to how much treatment is diagnosed.

We are a general and family practice. That means we see patients of every
age, from a first visit at around age one through to complex restorative
work in later life, and we handle most treatment in house rather than
referring it out.

## Continuing education

Every clinician here exceeds the state continuing education requirement of
fifteen hours per year. Both offices close for one Friday afternoon each
quarter for a whole-team training session, and those closures are posted on
the website and in the reminder texts at least a month in advance.

## Technology

Both offices use digital x-ray sensors rather than film. Digital sensors cut
radiation exposure by roughly eighty percent compared with the older film
systems, and the image is on screen immediately rather than needing
developing.

We use intraoral cameras routinely, and will show you what we are seeing on
the screen rather than just describing it. If we recommend treatment you are
entitled to see the image or the x-ray that supports the recommendation, and
you should ask if we have not shown you.

The Riverside office has a cone beam CT scanner, used for implant planning
and for assessing impacted teeth. A cone beam scan is one hundred and
ninety five dollars when it is not bundled into implant treatment.

Riverside also has the chairside milling unit used for same-day crowns. Oak
Park does not, so same-day crowns are only available at Riverside.

We use digital scanners instead of impression putty for crowns, night guards
and orthodontic cases at both offices. Most patients find the scan far more
comfortable than the old trays, particularly anyone with a strong gag reflex.

## Infection control

We follow CDC and OSHA guidance for dental settings. All handpieces are heat
sterilised between patients, not merely wiped down. Sterilisation equipment
is spore-tested weekly by an external laboratory and the results are kept on
file and available to any patient who asks to see them.

Single-use items are used once and discarded. Surfaces in the treatment room
are barrier-protected and disinfected between every patient.

## Sustainability

We are a paperless office for patient records and statements, other than for
patients who request paper. We recycle sterilisation wrap and paper products
and use an amalgam separator on every chair, which captures mercury waste
before it reaches the water system, as required by EPA rule.

## Complaints and feedback

If something goes wrong, tell us. Ask to speak to the office manager the same
day, or email feedback through the address on the website. We aim to
acknowledge every complaint within two business days and resolve it within
ten. If you remain unhappy you have the right to contact the North Carolina
State Board of Dental Examiners, and we will give you their contact details
if you ask; we would rather resolve it here first.

Reviews left online are read by the whole team. We never offer a discount, a
free service or any other incentive in exchange for a positive review, which
would be both against platform rules and against dental board guidance.
