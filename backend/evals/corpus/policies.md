# Brightleaf Family Dental - Office Policies

## Cancellations and rescheduling

We ask for at least twenty four hours notice to cancel or reschedule an
appointment. Notice given with less than twenty four hours, or a missed
appointment with no notice at all, is recorded as a late cancellation.

The first late cancellation or no-show in a twelve month period carries no
charge. We simply note it. The second and any subsequent occurrence within
the same twelve months is charged a fifty dollar fee. For appointments
longer than an hour, such as crowns, implants, oral surgery or a full
scaling and root planing, the missed appointment fee is one hundred dollars
rather than fifty.

The fee is charged to the card on file, or added to your account balance if
no card is on file. It is a practice fee and is never billed to insurance,
because no insurance plan covers a missed appointment.

Three no-shows in a twelve month period may result in being asked to move to
same-day-only scheduling, where we call you when a slot opens rather than
booking you in advance.

To cancel, call the front desk or reply to the text message reminder with
the word CANCEL. Cancelling through the reminder text is logged
automatically and is the most reliable method.

## Appointment reminders

We send a text message reminder seven days ahead and again one day ahead,
and an email reminder three days ahead. You can opt out of text reminders by
replying STOP, but doing so does not remove your responsibility to attend or
to cancel in time.

## Late arrivals

If you arrive up to ten minutes late we will normally still see you, though
the appointment may be shortened. If you arrive more than ten minutes late
the front desk will check with the clinical team, and whether we can still
see you depends entirely on what is booked after your slot. A shortened
appointment is not charged differently.

## Children and minors

Patients under eighteen must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian
for the first visit. For subsequent visits a minor aged fourteen to
seventeen may attend alone if we hold a signed consent form from the parent
or guardian, and if a parent or guardian is reachable by phone during the
appointment.

Patients under fourteen must be accompanied by an adult at every visit,
without exception.

A parent or guardian may accompany a child into the treatment room. For
children under seven we generally encourage it. For older children we
sometimes suggest the parent wait in the reception area, because many
children are noticeably calmer without an audience, but this is always the
family's choice and never a requirement.

If a child's parents are separated, the parent who brings the child to the
appointment is the parent who signs the consent for treatment that day.
We cannot mediate disagreements between parents about treatment, and we
cannot withhold records from a parent who holds legal custody rights.

## Records and privacy

Your dental records belong to you. You may request a copy of your records,
including x-rays, at any time. Written requests are fulfilled within ten
business days. There is no charge for the first copy of a record sent to you
or transferred to another dental office. A second or later copy within the
same year is charged a twenty five dollar administrative fee.

X-rays are transferred digitally at no cost. If you need them on physical
film there is a fifteen dollar charge per set.

We follow HIPAA. We will not discuss your care with a spouse, adult child,
family member or anyone else unless you have named them on the release form
in your chart. This applies even when the person calling is paying the bill.
The release form can be updated at any time at the front desk.

We do not sell patient data, ever, and we do not share it with marketing
partners. Anonymized and aggregated data is occasionally shared with our
dental support organization for quality reporting.

## Photography and imaging

We take clinical photographs as part of treatment planning for cosmetic and
orthodontic cases. These are stored in your chart. We ask separately, in
writing, before any image is used in marketing material or on our website,
and you may decline without any effect on your care. Consent to marketing
use can be withdrawn later, though we cannot recall printed material already
distributed.

## Refunds and treatment guarantees

Composite fillings placed by us are guaranteed for two years and crowns,
bridges and veneers for five years against fracture or failure under normal
use. If a restoration fails within that period we replace it at no charge,
provided you have kept your recommended recall visits.

The guarantee does not cover damage from trauma, from grinding when a
prescribed night guard has not been worn, or from decay caused by a lapse in
regular care. It does not transfer if you move to another practice.

Prepaid treatment that is not completed is refunded in full. Completed
treatment is not refundable, but if you are unhappy with the result we ask
that you tell us first and give us the chance to correct it.

## Antibiotic premedication

Patients who need antibiotic premedication before dental treatment,
typically those with certain heart conditions or joint replacements, are
responsible for taking the prescribed dose one hour before the appointment.
If you arrive and have not taken it, we will almost always have to
reschedule, and that reschedule is not counted as a late cancellation.

## Conduct

We ask that phones be silenced in treatment rooms. We are a scent-free
office out of consideration for staff and patients with sensitivities.
Abusive or threatening behaviour toward staff results in dismissal from the
practice, with emergency care provided for thirty days while you find
another provider.
