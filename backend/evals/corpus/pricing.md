# Brightleaf Family Dental - Fee Schedule

All prices below are our standard self-pay fees, effective January 2026.
These are the amounts charged to patients without insurance, or to insured
patients for procedures their plan does not cover. Patients with in-network
coverage will usually pay less; see the insurance and billing document.

Prices are per tooth or per visit as noted. A written estimate is provided
before any treatment over three hundred dollars begins.

## Preventive care

- New patient exam, including a full set of x-rays: $185
- Routine adult cleaning, also called a prophylaxis: $120
- Routine child cleaning, patients twelve and under: $95
- Periodic exam at a recurring six month visit: $65
- Bitewing x-rays, set of four: $75
- Single periapical x-ray: $35
- Full mouth series of x-rays: $160
- Panoramic x-ray: $145
- Fluoride varnish treatment: $45
- Dental sealant, per tooth: $60
- Oral cancer screening, when billed separately: $50

The new patient exam fee of $185 includes the x-rays but does not include a
cleaning. A first visit with both the exam and a cleaning is $305 in total.
This surprises people, so we say it clearly at booking.

## Fillings and basic restorative

- Composite tooth-colored filling, one surface: $210
- Composite filling, two surfaces: $265
- Composite filling, three or more surfaces: $320
- Amalgam silver filling, one surface: $175
- Temporary filling: $110
- Filling replacement, existing restoration: priced as a new filling

## Crowns, bridges and major restorative

- Porcelain crown: $1,350 per tooth
- Porcelain fused to metal crown: $1,240 per tooth
- Full gold crown: $1,480 per tooth
- Same-day milled ceramic crown: $1,495 per tooth
- Core buildup under a crown: $290
- Post and core: $340
- Three-unit bridge: $3,600
- Crown recementation: $165
- Inlay or onlay: $1,100

Same-day milled crowns cost more than lab crowns because the milling unit
and the ceramic block are more expensive than a lab case, but they remove
the need for a second visit and a temporary. Both carry the same five year
warranty against fracture under normal use.

## Root canals and oral surgery

- Root canal, front tooth: $950
- Root canal, premolar: $1,120
- Root canal, molar: $1,380
- Retreatment of a previous root canal: adds $400 to the above
- Simple extraction: $245
- Surgical extraction: $410
- Wisdom tooth extraction, erupted: $325 per tooth
- Wisdom tooth extraction, impacted: $520 per tooth
- Bone graft at extraction site: $650

A root canal is almost always followed by a crown on the same tooth, and
the crown is billed separately. A molar root canal plus a porcelain crown
therefore comes to $2,730 in total, which is the number we quote up front
rather than quoting the root canal alone.

## Implants

- Single implant fixture, surgical placement: $2,100
- Implant abutment: $650
- Implant crown: $1,590
- Total for a single tooth implant, all three stages: $4,340

Implant treatment runs over three to six months because the fixture has to
integrate with the bone before the crown is placed. The stages are billed as
they are completed, not all at the start.

## Periodontal treatment

- Scaling and root planing, per quadrant: $320
- Full mouth scaling and root planing, four quadrants: $1,180
- Periodontal maintenance cleaning: $165
- Gum graft, per site: $1,250

Periodontal maintenance at $165 replaces the routine $120 cleaning for
patients who have been treated for gum disease, and is typically needed
every three or four months rather than every six.

## Cosmetic

- In-office whitening, single session: $495
- Take-home whitening trays with gel: $320
- Porcelain veneer, per tooth: $1,450
- Composite bonding, per tooth: $390

Whitening is considered cosmetic and is not covered by any dental insurance
plan we work with. It is always a self-pay procedure.

## Orthodontics

- Orthodontic consultation: free
- Traditional braces, full treatment: $5,400
- Clear aligner treatment, full: $5,900
- Limited or single arch treatment: $3,200
- Retainer, per arch: $340
- Replacement retainer: $290

Orthodontic fees are quoted as a single total for the whole course of
treatment rather than per visit, and are almost always paid through a
monthly plan rather than up front. See the insurance and billing document
for how the monthly plans work.

## Emergency and after hours

- Emergency exam, limited problem-focused: $145
- After-hours emergency visit surcharge: $200
- Palliative treatment, pain relief only: $180

The after-hours surcharge of $200 is added on top of whatever treatment is
performed, and applies to visits outside normal office hours arranged
through the on-call line.
