# Brightleaf Family Dental - Scheduling and Frequently Asked Questions

## How far ahead to book

Routine cleanings book up four to six weeks ahead, and the Wednesday evening
and Saturday slots at Riverside go roughly three weeks earlier than that. If
you want a specific dentist, a specific hygienist or an evening slot, book
your next visit at checkout rather than waiting for the recall reminder.

Longer appointments, crowns, implant surgery and scaling and root planing,
generally have shorter waits than cleanings do, because we hold blocks in the
schedule for them.

## Same-day and short-notice appointments

We keep a cancellation list. If you ask to be on it, we will call when
something opens, which happens most days. People who can come at short notice
are often seen within a week even when the diary looks full.

Two urgent slots are held each weekday morning at Riverside for patients in
pain. These are not bookable online; call to be considered for one.

## Booking online

The online booking system handles routine cleanings and check-ups for
existing patients only. New patient visits, emergencies, and any treatment
appointment need a phone call, because the length of the slot and the room
depend on what is being done.

The online system shows availability for the next ninety days. If you need
something further out than that, call.

## Which office will I be seen at

Your confirmation email and reminder text both name the office in the subject
line. Riverside handles general dentistry, crowns, implants, oral surgery and
same-day crowns. Oak Park handles pediatric appointments, orthodontics and
routine general dentistry.

Remember that Oak Park is closed on Mondays and Riverside is open, which is
the most frequent source of confusion.

## How long will my appointment be

- Routine cleaning and exam: 45 minutes
- New patient first visit: 90 minutes
- Filling: 30 to 60 minutes
- Crown, first visit: 90 minutes
- Same-day crown: about 2 and a half hours
- Root canal, front tooth: about 1 hour
- Root canal, molar: 90 minutes to 2 hours
- Simple extraction: 20 to 30 minutes
- Scaling and root planing, two quadrants: about 1 hour
- Emergency exam: 30 minutes

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to have x-rays taken?** Not at every visit. Bitewings are usually
taken every twelve to twenty four months for adults depending on decay risk,
and a full series every three to five years. You can decline, though there
are things we genuinely cannot see without them, decay between teeth and bone
loss in particular.

**Can I see the dentist without a cleaning?** Yes. An exam alone is sixty
five dollars for an existing patient.

**Can I request a specific dentist?** Yes, and we will try to keep you with
the same clinician. Note that not every dentist works at both offices or
every day, so it may mean waiting longer for an appointment.

**Do you see patients with no insurance?** Yes. Roughly a third of our
patients are self-pay. The fee schedule is published and there is a payment
plan for treatment over one thousand dollars.

**Is there a discount for paying in full?** Yes. Treatment plans over two
thousand dollars paid in full at the start receive a five percent courtesy
discount. This cannot be combined with CareCredit or the in-house payment
plan.

**Do you offer a membership plan for uninsured patients?** Yes. The in-house
membership plan is three hundred and forty nine dollars per year for an
adult and covers two cleanings, two exams, one set of bitewings and an
emergency exam, plus fifteen percent off any other treatment. The child
membership is two hundred and forty nine dollars. It is not insurance, there
is no annual maximum and no waiting period, and it cannot be combined with
dental insurance.

**How often should I actually come in?** Every six months for most people.
Every three to four months if you have been treated for gum disease, if you
are diabetic, if you smoke, or if you have a high decay rate. Annually is
reasonable for a small number of very low risk adults, and we will tell you
if you are one.

**Do you take walk-ins?** For emergencies we will always try. For routine
care, call first; a walk-in for a cleaning will almost always be turned into
a booking for another day.

**Can I bring my children to my own appointment?** Yes, but there is no
supervision in the reception area, so children under about ten need an adult
with them who is not the person in the chair.

**Do you offer teledentistry?** We do short video consultations for triage,
for post-operative concerns and for orthodontic check-ins, at no charge for
existing patients. A video call cannot replace an examination, and anything
involving pain or swelling will end with us asking you to come in.

**What if I have not been to a dentist in ten years?** You are not unusual
and you will not be lectured. Book a first visit, tell us at booking that it
has been a long time, and we will schedule extra time. We will lay out
everything we find, sort it into what is urgent and what can wait, and work
through it at whatever pace suits you and your budget.

**Do you treat patients with disabilities or sensory sensitivities?** Yes.
Both offices are wheelchair accessible, and Dr. Raman has additional training
in treating patients with sensory sensitivities and autism. Tell us what
helps and what does not when you book, and we will plan the visit around it,
including quiet times of day and pre-visit familiarisation appointments.
